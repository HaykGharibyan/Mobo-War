MOBO WAR · DATA CARE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mobo War handles information when you use the mobile game and its related support services.

1. Who operates the game

Mobo War is operated by the Mobo War development team. For privacy questions, contact ustapisoo@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, selected units, weapons, skins, player name, missions, rewards, and battle results locally on your device. This information is used to save progress and provide the game features.

3. Remote analytics and crash diagnostics

Mobo War currently does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or another remote telemetry service. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

4. Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. The game does not receive or store your payment-card number. Google Play and purchase-verification services may process product IDs, transaction identifiers, purchase status, and related technical information to complete and validate a purchase.

5. Support messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your email, such as your message, app version, device details, and screenshots. We use it only to answer the request, investigate bugs, and improve the game.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information, and the game does not transmit gameplay data to an analytics provider. Google Play processes purchases, and support messages are sent to the email address you choose when contacting support.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data can be removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data. You may request deletion of information held in a support conversation by emailing ustapisoo@gmail.com.

8. Children

Mobo War is not specifically directed to children under 13. Do not enter passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information into game fields. If you are a minor, use the game with permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.
