Support

Need help with Mobo War? Send us a message and include enough detail for us to investigate the issue.

Email support

What to include

  • Your device model and Android version
  • The game version shown in the app
  • The level or screen where the problem happened
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • A screenshot or short video, if useful

Common help

Make sure the game is updated to the latest version and restart it once after an update. For purchase questions, include the Google Play order number but never send your card number, password, or service-account JSON.
